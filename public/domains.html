Connect your domain

One DNS record, and every address on your domain becomes a mailbox here. There is no second step: no account, no verification page, no key to keep. The first message that arrives is what connects the domain.

  1. 1

    Add this MX record at your DNS provider

    TypeMX
    Name@
    Priority10
    Value — the part you paste smtp.neverla.web.id

    Delete every other MX record on the domain. Mail can only be delivered to one place, and a leftover record from a previous provider takes part of your traffic in silence. We accept a domain only when we are its single MX, for exactly that reason.

  2. 2

    There is no step two

    Send anything to any address on your domain. We look the MX up as the message arrives; if it points here, the domain is connected on the spot and that first message is kept — not bounced, not lost while something is activated. Read it at https://mail.neverla.web.id/inbox/anything@yourdomain.com or through the same API as everything else.

Read this before you point a domain here

Mail on your domain is readable by anyone who knows the address, exactly like our public domains — there is no key and no password on it. That is the trade for having no account: nothing identifies an owner, so nothing can be locked to one. Use it for throwaway signups, for testing, for an address you print on a page. Do not use it for anything you would mind a stranger reading.

What you get

  • Every address on the domain, created on the fly
  • Working from the first message — nothing to activate
  • Your domain never appears anywhere on this site
  • The same API, and the same 5-day retention
  • Free, like the rest of it
